1. **Iteration 0 — Preparation:** finalize structure and `context/context-base.json` for prompt grounding.
2. **Iteration 1 — Identity & Narrative:** build short/medium/full bios and core pillars in `biography/`.
3. **Iteration 2 — Career Timeline:** document roles with impacts and lessons in `career/`.
4. **Iteration 3 — Skills:** map technical and leadership skills plus matrix artifacts in `skills/`.
5. **Iteration 4 — Projects:** capture context, architecture, tech, and impact for key projects in `projects/`.
6. **Iteration 5 — Philosophy:** articulate principles, leadership style, and architect mindset in `philosophy/`.
7. **Iteration 6 — Community & Content:** record talks, workshops, and contributions in `community/` and `speaking/`.
8. **Iteration 7 — Interests & Vision:** detail interests and a five-year vision in `interests/` and `biography/vision.md`.
9. **Iteration 8 — Context Engine:** consolidate grounding JSON and prompt templates in `context/`.
10. **Iteration 9 — Content CI:** add linting/validation workflows and contribution guidelines.
11. **Iteration 10 — Polishing:** create final README, site index, and optional GitHub Pages under `/docs`.
