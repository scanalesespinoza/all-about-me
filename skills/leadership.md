# Leadership & Non-Technical Skills — Sergio Sebastián Canales Espinoza

This document describes my leadership, communication, and organizational skills, and how they complement my technical experience.

---

## 1. Technical Leadership & Mentoring

**Key Themes**
- Growing people, not just systems
- Coaching architects, developers, and platform engineers
- Leading by example and clarity

**Skills**
- Mentoring engineers and architects to improve their technical depth and decision-making.
- Facilitating architectural discussions and helping teams reason about trade-offs.
- Setting standards of excellence (e.g., NPS 9–10 as a baseline) in delivery and customer satisfaction.
- Providing constructive feedback focused on growth, not blame.

**How I Use This**
- Turn architecture from a gatekeeping function into an enabling practice.
- Build councils of experts that provide integrated oversight across initiatives.
- Help teams understand where they are, where they can go, and how to get there.

## 2. Communication & Stakeholder Management

**Key Themes**
- Clear, calm, and contextual communication
- Bridging technical and business perspectives
- Avoiding dogmatic or ego-driven narratives

**Skills**
- Communicating complex technical topics in a way that is accessible to non-technical stakeholders.
- Working with C-level stakeholders to align architecture and platform strategy with business goals.
- Facilitating sessions where teams and leaders co-create roadmaps and priorities.

**Key Themes**
- Continuous improvement instead of “big bang transformation”
- Aligning sub-goals with enterprise value
- Connecting architecture, delivery, and operations

**Skills**
- Defining and evolving architectural and delivery practices over time.
- Connecting platform capabilities, delivery processes, and business objectives.
- Using metrics (e.g., NPS, reliability, time-to-value) to guide improvements.
