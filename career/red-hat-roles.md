# Red Hat Roles — Sergio Sebastián Canales Espinoza

This document expands on my journey within Red Hat, focusing on responsibilities, impact, and evolution from consultant to senior architect. Titles and dates follow [the career timeline](timeline.md).

I have worked at Red Hat in two stages: February 2012 – March 2013, and continuously since November 2014.

---

## First Stage

### Associate Consultant (February 2012 – March 2013)

**Scope:**
- Consulting delivery on Red Hat technologies.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Produce reference architectures, proofs of concept, and designs.
- Deliver performance enhancements, documentation, product patches, and other artifacts as needed.

---

## Second Stage

### Senior Consultant (November 2014 – August 2021)

**Scope:**
- Red Hat hybrid cloud business: OpenShift Container Platform, application runtimes, and automation.
- Strategic clients with enterprise middleware needs.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design middleware solutions that meet each client's needs, acting as a trusted advisor for business goals.
- Serve as the communication channel between product teams, product engineering, support, professional services, and sales.
- Deliver architecture definitions, component and service integration, proofs of concept, technical validations, workshops, and platform installation and configuration.

---

### Application Development and Practices Architect (September 2021 – August 2022)

**Scope:**
- Container platforms and cloud-native application delivery.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design and implement modern container platforms.
- Improve customers' software delivery workflows.
- Recommend cloud-native development practices with Linux containers, Kubernetes, Ansible Automation Platform, RHEL CoreOS, and OpenShift.

---

### Senior Architect – App, Dev & Practices (August 2022 – October 2024)

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Mentor and coach teams while engaging stakeholders across business and engineering.

**Key Responsibilities:**
