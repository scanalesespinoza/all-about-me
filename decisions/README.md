# Decision Log — Sergio Sebastián Canales Espinoza

Real decisions I have made, with my own reasons. Agents use these cases to compare a new situation with how I actually decided before. Cases matter more than adjectives: a trait says what I value; a case shows what I gave up for it.

How agents use this directory is described in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). How to test an agent's predictions is described in [evaluation.md](evaluation.md).

---

## Rules

- Record only decisions I actually made, with reasons I stated. Do not reconstruct my motives from the timeline.
- Mark any interpretation added by someone else as interpretation.
- Include what I rejected and what it cost me; that is what makes a case useful for prediction.
- Record decisions that contradict my usual profile as well. They calibrate the profile.
- Keep career titles and dates consistent with [the career timeline](../career/timeline.md).
- Do not include private contact data, confidential customer details, or other people's personal information.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-short-topic.md`, for example `2026-09-global-services-role.md`.

## Template

```markdown
# Decision: [short title]

**Date:** YYYY-MM
**Domain:** [career / project / community / technical / other]
**Source:** [stated by me / interpretation]

## Context
What was happening and what forced a decision.

## Options
1. Option A — what it offered and what it cost.
2. Option B — what it offered and what it cost.

## What I Chose
The option, in one sentence.

## Why
My reasons, in my own words.

## What I Gave Up
The cost I accepted.

## Traits and Criteria Involved
Which traits or criteria from philosophy/personality-and-decision-criteria.md drove the choice, and which ones lost.

## Outcome
What happened afterwards, and whether I would decide the same way today.
```
