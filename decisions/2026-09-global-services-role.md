# Decision: Move to the global services team

**Date:** 2026-09
**Domain:** Career
**Source:** Reasons stated by me. The alternative option is an interpretation based on [the career timeline](../career/timeline.md).

## Context

From October 2024 I was Associate Principal Architect at Red Hat, working on organization-level technology strategy. In September 2026 I became Senior Architect – Global Services, joining the global team.

## Options

1. **Remain Associate Principal Architect** — continue in a known role and scope. *(Interpretation: the alternative implied by the timeline.)*
2. **Join the global services team** — compete and work alongside the best professionals in the company.

## What I Chose

Joining the global services team as Senior Architect – Global Services.

## Why

I want to reach the top among professionals at Red Hat and be the best in my professional context. Going to the global team lets me measure myself against the best and grow until I become a reference: indisputably among the best in the company, and then in the world.

## What I Gave Up

Not stated. On paper the move went from an "Associate Principal" title to a "Senior" title. An agent must not read it as a step back: the reason was the level of the people I measure myself against, not the title.

## Traits and Criteria Involved

- **Self-improvement** (priority 3): measuring myself against the best was the explicit driver.
- **Competition as self-measurement:** the competition was with the best, as a path to growth, not winning for its own sake (winning ranks last).
- **New over predictable:** a new team and scope instead of continuing the known role.
- Title and status were not decisive.

## Outcome

Too early to assess: the move happened in September 2026.
