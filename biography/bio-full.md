# Full Bio — Sergio Sebastián Canales Espinoza

Sergio Sebastián Canales Espinoza is a Principal Architect at Red Hat with over 12 years of experience leading Cloud Native, Platform Engineering, and Enterprise Architecture initiatives across Latin America. Recognized globally since 2024 as both a CNCF Ambassador and Platform Engineering Ambassador, he is an influential voice in cloud-native ecosystems and a committed builder of open communities.

His work focuses on designing scalable, human-centered platforms that empower developers and enable organizations to evolve sustainably. Sergio believes that real progress comes from continuous improvement—not from forced “transformations”—and that the growth of individuals is what ultimately drives the evolution of systems, platforms, and enterprises.

Throughout his career, he has guided organizations in modernizing their architectures, implementing Kubernetes and OpenShift platforms, improving delivery practices, and enabling teams to adopt DevOps, GitOps, and developer-centered workflows. His experience spans advisory roles with C-level stakeholders, hands-on architecture, and leading cross-functional teams across LATAM.

Sergio is also known for pioneering AI-augmented development within enterprise environments. He has delivered production-ready MVPs in just two weeks using agentic workflows and tools like ChatGPT Codex, Quarkus, and Kubernetes—demonstrating how AI and cloud-native engineering can accelerate business value while maintaining excellence and reliability.

Beyond his technical contributions, Sergio is deeply committed to community building. He is the founder of Open Source Santiago, organizer of DevOpsDays Santiago, and a frequent speaker across conferences and meetups. His work centers on empowering people, connecting communities, and strengthening LATAM’s role in the global open-source ecosystem.

Calm, reflective, and purpose-driven, Sergio communicates with clarity and humility. He avoids absolutist statements and ego-driven narratives, preferring instead to offer context, structure, and insight so others can think freely. His leadership style is rooted in respect, continuous learning, and a genuine belief in the potential of people.

Today, Sergio continues to help organizations architect future-ready platforms, adopt AI responsibly, and cultivate teams capable of delivering sustainable value. His vision is simple: build technology that strengthens people, communities, and the organizations that rely on them.
