# Bio — Full Version

I specialize in designing resilient, human-centered platforms that empower developers and deliver measurable business value. My background combines hands-on engineering with strategic guidance: building Internal Developer Platforms with Kubernetes and OpenShift, standardizing GitOps and DevSecOps practices, and mentoring architecture teams to scale consistent excellence.

I co-founded Open Source Santiago, organize DevOpsDays Santiago, and regularly speak on topics like AI-augmented development, platform maturity models, and developer experience. I’ve delivered production-ready MVPs in two weeks by pairing cloud-native patterns with AI copilots, and I help enterprises evolve their governance and operating models to sustain that speed.

I work best where architecture, delivery, and operations intersect. My approach is calm and structured, balancing vision with pragmatic delivery. I believe in continuous improvement rather than one-off transformation, and I lead with empathy, transparency, and data-driven decision-making. Ultimately, my goal is to amplify human potential through technology—creating ecosystems where AI, open-source, and disciplined craftsmanship coexist to make organizations more capable, ethical, and innovative.
